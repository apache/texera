About ADMAP

The Alzheimer's Disease Multimodal Atlasing Project (ADMAP) is a collaborative initiative at the UCI Center for Neural Circuit Mapping. We advance Alzheimer's disease research by providing a comprehensive platform for researchers to upload, explore, and download multimodal datasets.

Key Objectives

Brain Circuit Characterization

Comprehensively characterize brain circuits susceptible to Alzheimer's disease type pathology and neurodegeneration in mouse models of AD

Data Integration

Share data on the cellular connectome in aging and AD brains within a common reference atlas integrating molecular and anatomical annotations

Connectivity Mapping

Map connectivity changes between cells in aging and AD to understand mechanisms underlying resilience and vulnerability

Vulnerable Cell Types

Extend research on vulnerable cell types to provide greater understanding of AD progression and potential therapeutic targets
